Senior Full Stack Developer

About Awesomic:

Awesomic is a rapidly expanding startup with a team of over 120 professionals, recognized as the fastest growing startup in Ukraine. With significant investments from Google, Y Combinator, Michael Siebel of Twitch, and Ragnar Sass of Pipedrive, Awesomic is on the path to becoming the next unicorn


Role Summary:

We are seeking a talented Senior Full-Stack Developer to join our dynamic team. You will have the opportunity to lead the technical development of various exciting projects (including a marketplace app, a cutting-edge platform for designers, a SaaS app in the hiring space, and more). In this role, you will guide and manage middle developers, ensuring the successful delivery of high-quality solutions. Your expertise will be critical in driving our projects forward and shaping the future of our technology.


Key Responsibilities:

Lead and maintain web applications using PHP (Laravel) and React;
Take early-stage technical leadership roles;
Mentor middle developers and manage their contributions;
Focus on high detail in UI/UX integration and performance;
Manage complex application architectures and database interactions;
Implement and uphold high-quality OOP practices and patterns.
 

Skills and Qualifications:

Over 5 years of full-stack development experience in PHP, Laravel, and React;
Demonstrated ability to lead projects and manage development teams;
Proficient in HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, MySQL, and API integration;
Strong skills in version control using Git;
Expertise in managing complex application architectures and OOP;
Experience in startups or personal projects is highly regarded.

 

If you’re skilled in full-stack development with other technologies and eager to learn PHP, Laravel, and React, we’d love to have you on our team! 

Benefits:

You’ll join the coolest community of goal-driven people who love what they do and are ready to change the game with innovative decisions;
You’ll have the opportunity to work closely with Roman Sevast and Stacy Pavlyshyna, Founders of Awesomic, YC alumni, and Forbes 30 Under 30 honorees;
You’ll have unlimited prospects to develop as a professional;
You’ll benefit from a mental health support program, a work laptop (MacBook), unlimited vacation days, coverage for courses and other learning activities, health insurance, and the ability to work in a hub in Warsaw, Kyiv, Lviv, or fully remote.
 

While our primary stack includes PHP, Laravel, and React, we also value experience with other technologies such as Python, Node.js, Ruby, and front-end frameworks like Vue and Angular. We are open to candidates who have a strong foundation in these areas and are eager to learn and adapt to our stack quickly.
